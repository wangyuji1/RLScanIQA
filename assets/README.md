# Figure provenance

The figures in this directory are curated previews from the authors' RL-ScanIQA paper and supplementary project materials. They are included to explain the method and reported qualitative behavior; no raw benchmark dataset is redistributed.

- `poster_preview.jpg`: compressed preview of the public CVPR 2026 poster.
- `figures/viewport_grid.png`: candidate viewport tiling over an ERP image.
- `figures/learned_scanpaths.png`: representative learned scanpaths and selected viewports.
- `figures/mos_predictions.png`: reported MOS and prediction examples.
- `figures/distortion_augmentations.png`: weak, mild, and strong augmentation examples.

Dataset images remain subject to their original benchmark terms. If you are a rights holder and believe an illustration should be removed, please open an issue with the relevant filename.
